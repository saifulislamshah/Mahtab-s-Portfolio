AI Visibility Platform · Video Content · Motion Graphics

ABOUT THE BRAND

CiteRise is a modern AI visibility platform built specifically for attorneys and law firms. The brand focuses on helping legal professionals become more visible and recommended across AI platforms by strengthening the sources, content, and signals that AI systems trust.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Create engaging video content that communicates CiteRise's AI-powered approach in a clear and accessible way. The videos were designed to explain the platform's value, highlight its capabilities, and make complex concepts around AI visibility and legal marketing easier to understand.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Clean Compositions → Motion Graphics → Visual Storytelling → Dynamic Typography

Smooth transitions, dynamic typography, and purposeful animation were used to transform information-heavy concepts into engaging visual content while maintaining a professional and trustworthy look.

PROSIGNALS AI

Hero Section Video · Trading Platform · Motion Design

ABOUT THE BRAND

ProSignals AI is an AI-powered trading platform focused on helping traders access market insights, trading signals, and educational resources. The platform combines advanced AI systems with experienced human traders to analyse market conditions and identify potential trading opportunities across markets such as gold, forex, and other assets.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Create a visually engaging hero section video that immediately communicates the platform's modern, technology-driven approach to trading. The video needed to capture attention from the first moment while visually supporting the brand's focus on AI, market analysis, and intelligent trading technology.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Premium Visual Style → Dynamic Motion → Financial Visuals → Smooth Transitions

The hero video was designed to create an impactful first impression while complementing the website's interface and reinforcing ProSignals AI's positioning as a modern AI-assisted trading platform.

Hero Video Production Motion Design Visual Effects Financial Visuals Creative Direction
